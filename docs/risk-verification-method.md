# Risk Verification Method

CZB Security Lab treats risk verification as an evidence labeling process, not a certainty engine.

## Confidence Levels

- Confirmed: directly supported by public chain data or official records.
- Likely: supported by multiple signals but not definitive.
- Watch: relevant signal exists, but evidence is incomplete.
- Unknown: no reliable conclusion yet.

## Risk Flags

- Fresh outbound drain pattern
- Suspicious approval or spender
- Known phishing domain reference
- Rapid fan-out across many addresses
- Exchange deposit or service interaction
- Bridge or swap path requiring deeper review

## Reviewer Discipline

- Record source URLs.
- Separate facts from inference.
- Use UTC timestamps.
- Redact victim-sensitive data.
- Avoid attacker attribution without strong evidence.
